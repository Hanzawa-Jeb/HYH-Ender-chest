\fbox{a is Congruent to b modulo m的意思就是a与b能够整除以m，b-a = km}

- if a = dq + r, then a->dividend, q->quotient, d->divisor, r->remainder
## Modular arithmetic
$$
\fbox{if a $\equiv$ b (mod m) and c $\equiv$ d (mod m), then ac $\equiv$ bd (mod m)}
$$
- （a+b）mod m = ((a mod m) + (b mod m)) mod m
- ab mod m = ((a mod m)(b mod m)) mod m
